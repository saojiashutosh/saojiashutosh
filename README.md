## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 1. [Skiwa](https://github.com/saojiashutosh/Skiwa)
**Full-Stack E-commerce Platform**

A modern e-commerce platform built for a premium streetwear brand, featuring product management, authentication, shopping cart, checkout and payment integration.

**Tech:** Next.js, TypeScript, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Razorpay, Cloudinary, Docker.

### 2. [Soul-Space](https://github.com/saojiashutosh/Soul-Space)
**Digital Wellbeing & Productivity App**

A native Android application designed to help users improve digital habits through app blocking, focus timers, meditation, ambient sounds, habit tracking and hydration reminders.

**Tech:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room Database, DataStore, Coroutines, WorkManager.

### 3. [Harmonic Navigator](https://github.com/saojiashutosh/harmonic-navigator)
**Context-Aware Music Recommendation System**

An intelligent music discovery platform that recommends songs and playlists based on a user's mood and current context rather than relying solely on listening history.

**Tech:** Python, Django REST Framework, React, PostgreSQL, Redis, AI-powered recommendation engine.

### 4. [Travistor](https://github.com/saojiashutosh/Travistor)
**Travel & Tour Booking Platform**

A travel platform that helps users explore and book personalized travel packages, including Women Special and Family Special categories. Also features wishlists and chatbot integration.

**Tech:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Botpress.

### 5. [ShutterCart](https://github.com/saojiashutosh/ShutterCart)
**Camera & Accessories E-commerce**

A Django-based online shopping platform focused on cameras and photography accessories, bringing product discovery and e-commerce functionality together.

**Tech:** Python, Django, HTML, CSS.

### 6. [BlogPost](https://github.com/saojiashutosh/BlogPost)
**Django Blogging Application**

A blogging application where users can create, update and delete blog posts using Django forms, model forms and database-backed functionality.

**Tech:** Python, Django, SQLite, HTML, CSS.
